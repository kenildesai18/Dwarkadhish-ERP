# Vyapar & Stock Manager (CommerceHub Pro ERP)

A modern, fast, and comprehensive web application built for online e-commerce sellers (Meesho, Amazon, Flipkart, Shopify) and wholesale businesses with a **live 2-partner accounting and settlement ledger**.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Dashboard & Live Analytics**
   - **Total Stock Valuation**: Instant real-time valuation of all godown inventory based on cost price.
   - **Total Business Expenses**: Month-to-date and lifetime business operational costs.
   - **Wholesale Receivables (Market Due)**: Track outstanding customer credit with 1-click payment collection.
   - **Supplier Payables (Vendor Due)**: Track unpaid purchase bills with 1-click vendor settlement logging.

2. **Inventory & Multi-Price Stock Management**
   - Cost Price, Online Retail Price, and Wholesale B2B Price for every product.
   - Live auto-deduction on sales and auto-addition on purchase bills.
   - **Stock Adjustments (+/-)**: Easily record customer returns (RTO), damages, audits, and giveaways.
   - Low stock alerts with automated visual badges.

3. **Multi-Channel Sales Register**
   - Support for Meesho, Amazon, Flipkart, Website, and Wholesale B2B clients.
   - Status tracking: Fully Paid, Partial Paid, or Pending / Credit.
   - **Printable Tax Invoices & Receipts** with professional layout.

4. **Purchase Bills & Inward Management**
   - Record purchases with supplier name, bill number, and items received.
   - Track payment status (Paid, On Credit / Pending, Partial) and record which partner made the payment.

5. **Daily Expense Tracker**
   - Categorized expenses: Packaging Materials, Courier & Shipping, Refreshments, Rent, Utilities, Salaries, Transport.
   - Track who paid out-of-pocket (Partner 1, Partner 2, or Business Account).

6. **2-Partner Accounting & Automated Settlement Engine**
   - Tracks total out-of-pocket contributions (Purchases + Expenses + Capital Injected - Direct Settlements).
   - Configurable profit/expense sharing ratio (default 50-50%).
   - **Live Settlement Verdict**: Automatically calculates who owes whom and how much to balance the accounts.

7. **Reports & 1-Click Excel Export**
   - Master Excel Workbook (.xlsx) with all 5 sheets (Inventory, Sales, Purchases, Expenses, Partner Ledger).
   - Individual Excel downloads for each module.
   - JSON Database Backup & Restore.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

1. Open your browser and navigate to:
   ```
   http://localhost:3000
   ```
2. Press `Ctrl + F5` to ensure the latest styles and scripts are loaded.
